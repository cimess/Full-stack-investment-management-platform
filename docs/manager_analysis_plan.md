# Professional Investor Analysis Suite Roadmap

This document outlines the strategic implementation of a high-end investment analysis platform, designed to attract institutional managers and professional investors.

## 1. Core Analytical Pillars

### A. Fundamental Analysis (The "Intelligent Investor" Approach)
Focuses on the intrinsic value of an asset based on financial statements and economic factors.
- **Key Metrics:** P/E Ratio, P/B Ratio, Debt-to-Equity, Free Cash Flow, Dividend Yield.
- **Advanced Features:** 
    - **DCF (Discounted Cash Flow) Modeler:** Allow managers to input growth assumptions and see "fair value" estimates.
    - **Peer Comparison:** Side-by-side analysis of a stock against its sector competitors.
    - **Historical Breakdown:** 5-10 year trends in revenue and profit margins.

### B. Technical Analysis (The "Quant" Approach)
Uses historical price action and volume to predict future movements.
- **Indicators:** RSI (Relative Strength Index), MACD, Bollinger Bands, Fibonacci Retracements.
- **Advanced Features:**
    - **Interactive Candlestick Charts:** Multi-timeframe views (1m to 1Y).
    - **Pattern Recognition:** AI-driven detection of "Head and Shoulders," "Cup and Handle," etc.
    - **Volume Profile:** Understanding at what price levels the most trading happened.

### C. Sentiment Analysis (The "Social" Edge)
Gauging the market mood through news and social media.
- **Sources:** X (Twitter), Reddit (r/wallstreetbets), News Headlines, Analyst Ratings.
- **Advanced Features:**
    - **Fear & Greed Index:** A consolidated score of market emotion.
    - **Trending Tickers:** Heatmap of what's being discussed most right now.
    - **Analyst Distribution:** Visualizing "Buy/Hold/Sell" consensus from top banks.

## 5. Portfolio Analytics & Risk Management (The "Manager's Armor")
Professional managers don't just look at returns; they look at **Risk-Adjusted Returns**.

### A. Performance Ratios
Metrics that tell you if the profit was worth the risk.
- **Sharpe Ratio:** Reward per unit of total risk. A score > 1.0 is "Good," > 2.0 is "Very Good."
- **Sortino Ratio:** Like Sharpe, but only penalizes *downward* volatility (perfect for professional investors).
- **Alpha & Beta:** 
    - **Beta:** How sensitive the portfolio is to the general market (S&P 500).
    - **Alpha:** The "excess return" generated by the manager's skill.

### B. Risk Exposure Tools
- **VaR (Value at Risk):** A statistical estimate of the maximum loss a portfolio could take within a certain time frame (e.g., "95% chance we won't lose more than $5k today").
- **Correlation Matrix:** A heatmap showing if all assets move together. Managers use this to ensure **Diversification** (if one drops, others should stay stable).
- **Drawdown Analysis:** Visualizing the "peak to valley" decline. Professional managers obsess over minimizing drawdowns.

## 9. Global Macro Intelligence (The "Big Picture")
Professional investors look at the "Macro" before they look at individual stocks.

### A. The Yield Curve (Recession Predictor)
- **Concept:** The difference between the 10-year and 2-year Treasury yields. When this "inverts" (goes negative), it has predicted almost every recession in modern history.
- **Implementation:** Fetch Treasury data from **St. Louis Fed (FRED API)**.

### B. Currency Strength (DXY - The Dollar Index)
- **Concept:** When the US Dollar is strong, it usually puts pressure on Stocks and Gold. 
- **Package:** Use the same Market Data API to track the `DXY` ticker.

### C. Inflation & Interest Rate Calendar
- **Concept:** The "Fed Meeting" and "CPI Report" are the two biggest events every month.
- **Feature:** A countdown clock to the next Federal Reserve interest rate decision.

## 10. Quantitative Finance Formulas (The "Math")
To build a "Professional Grade" backend, you need these formulas calculated in your services:

### A. Sharpe Ratio
Ratio of (Average Return - Risk-Free Rate) / (Standard Deviation of returns).
- *Goal:* Higher is better. >1.0 is the benchmark for a pro manager.

### B. Beta (Correlation to Market)
Covariance(Stock, Market) / Variance(Market).
- *Goal:* 1.0 means it moves with the market. >1.5 means it's "volatile" (high risk/reward). 

### C. Max Drawdown
The largest percentage drop from a peak to a trough.
- *Goal:* Managers must keep this low to avoid "Panicking" their investors.

## 11. Institutional Order Flow (The "Whale" Tracker)
This shows you what is happening *inside* the market before the price moves.

### A. Level 2 Data & Order Book
- **Concept:** Seeing all the "Buy" orders and "Sell" orders waiting in the queue. 
- **Visual:** A "Depth Chart" (looks like two mountains meeting). 
- **Why it matters:** It shows where the big "Support" and "Resistance" levels are located.

### B. Time & Sales (The "Tape")
- **Concept:** A scrolling list of every single trade happening in real-time. 
- **Professional Edge:** Highlighting "Large Block Trades" (e.g., when a bank buys $5M of Apple at once).
