We are going to use the exact Unlevered Free Cash Flow (FCFF) corporate finance formulas taught by the Corporate Finance Institute (CFI) and Aswath Damodaran (the "Dean of Valuation" at NYU Stern). This is the exact math used by investment banking analysts at Goldman Sachs and J.P. Morgan.

The Certified Wall Street Formulas (Top-Down)

Revenue Forecast = Last Year's Revenue * (1 + Revenue Growth Rate)
Operating Income (EBIT) = Revenue * Target Operating Margin
Taxes = Operating Income * Effective Tax Rate (Usually 21% for US companies)
NOPAT (Net Operating Profit After Taxes) = Operating Income - Taxes
FCFF (Free Cash Flow to Firm) = NOPAT (This assumes Depreciation roughly equals Capital Expenditures, which is standard for top-line modelers).
Present Value = FCFF / (1 + WACC)^Year
